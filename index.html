Our first app

Pooled for Android

Pooled, shared money made simple

Pooled helps flatmates, families and friends track daily spending, share a space and see what everyone has left. No account needed to start.

Explore Pooled sunnyorbit.com/pooled

  • Track daily spending

    Log everyday costs, set a budget per category and see where the money goes.

  • Share a space

    Make a space for your home, family or a trip and invite people with a six-character code.

  • See what everyone has left

    Send money ahead into a space. Every member has a live balance as it is spent.

  • Private by default

    No ads and no tracking. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone.

What we build

Built for everyday life

SunnyOrbit makes small mobile apps that solve ordinary problems well. No clutter, no pressure, no tricks.

Built for real daily life

Pooled keeps everyday money clear, on your own or in a shared space with flatmates, family or a partner.

Your data stays yours

Your ledger and receipt photos are encrypted on your phone, with the key kept in the Android Keystore.

No ads, no tracking

No ads, no analytics and no tracking SDK. Pooled never reads your advertising ID.

No account needed

Start straight away, with no sign-up. Making an account is optional.

Free in early access

No price and no subscription. There is nothing to pay while Pooled is in early access.

Designed for Android

Four home-screen widgets, an app lock with PIN, password or fingerprint, and nine languages. Runs on Android 7.0 and above.

How we build

Four habits shape every SunnyOrbit app, from the first sketch to each update that reaches your phone.

01

Start with a real problem

We build after seeing the same frustration again and again, never because a feature sounds clever.

02

Keep it simple to use

One clear screen beats five busy ones. If a step is not needed, it goes.

03

Respect privacy and attention

We ask for the least data we need. Pooled works with no account at all, and shows no ads.

04

Ship, listen, improve

Small, careful updates, and a real reply to the people who write to us.
