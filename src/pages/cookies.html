1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Sites use them to remember things about you, such as a login or a preference, or to follow you from page to page and from site to site.

There are similar technologies that do the same job without being cookies: local storage, session storage and other browser databases, tracking pixels, and “fingerprinting” that recognises your device from its settings. When this policy says we use no cookies, it means none of these either.

2. What this site uses

Nothing. sunnyorbitapps.com does not set cookies and does not store or read anything on your device. In particular, there is:

  • no cookie of any kind, including “essential” ones;
  • no local storage, session storage or other browser storage;
  • no analytics, statistics or visitor counting;
  • no advertising, tracking pixels or social media buttons;
  • nothing loaded from another company. Our fonts, icons and images are served from this site, so opening a page does not contact Google Fonts or any other third party.

Because nothing is stored, there is no cookie banner and nothing to accept or decline.

You can check this yourself. Open your browser’s developer tools (usually F12, or right-click and choose “Inspect”), go to the Application or Storage tab and look under Cookies and Local Storage for sunnyorbitapps.com: both will be empty. The Network tab will show that every file comes from this site.

3. The one script on this site

Our pages load one small script of our own, assets/js/site.js. It does three things:

  • opens and closes the menu on small screens;
  • fades sections in as you scroll (and switches this off if your device asks for less motion);
  • on the contact page, turns the form into an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent from the page itself.

The script stores nothing and reads nothing from your device. Every page works without it.

4. Our hosting provider

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. GitHub does not set cookies on this site, but like any web server it sees the IP address of each visitor. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. We do not receive or use those logs.

You can read more in the GitHub Pages documentation (under “Data collection”) and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. Links to other sites

Some pages link to other sites, such as the Pooled listing on Google Play. Nothing from those sites is embedded here. If you follow a link, you are on their site, and their cookie and privacy policies apply there.

6. Cookies and our apps

This policy covers the sunnyorbitapps.com website. Pooled is an Android app, not a website, and it uses no cookies. It has no ads, no analytics and no advertising ID. What Pooled stores, and where, is explained in the Pooled Privacy Policy.

7. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Your browser may send a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. This site does not track you or sell or share your information, with or without that signal, so there is nothing for it to switch off. You are welcome to leave it on.
