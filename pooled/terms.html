5. What Pooled is, and what it is not

Pooled is a record-keeping tool. It records amounts you enter yourself. It does not lend money, arrange or broker loans, process payments, transfer or hold money, check credit, or give financial advice. It is not a bank, a payment service or a lender, and it holds no funds. “Sending” an amount inside the app moves a number in a ledger and nothing else. Any real payment between you and another person happens outside Pooled and is entirely between you.

Lent & borrowed records debts that already exist; Pooled never moves money. Amounts you record there are notes between you and people you know. Pooled does not offer, arrange or underwrite credit of any kind, and it does not verify or enforce what you record.

Balances, budgets and reports are calculated from what you and the members of your spaces entered. We do not check them. They are not financial, tax, accounting or legal advice, and they are not evidence of a legally enforceable debt. Check anything you are going to rely on, especially before paying somebody or filing anything official.

9. How to report, and what happens

In the app, open a shared space, go to Members, and use the menu beside a person's name, or use the menu on somebody else's entry. Report asks which of the five things above is wrong and then does two things:

  • It blocks that person for you, straight away.
  • It writes the report out so you can send it on, through your phone's share sheet: to the owner of the space, who can remove a member, or to us at support@sunnyorbitapps.com.

We will not pretend otherwise: there is no moderation desk and no queue, and the app does not send reports to us by itself. We do not read ledgers, so we only know about a problem when somebody tells us. When you send us a report, we look at what you sent. If we need to, we may look at the specific content it points to, in the cloud copy of that space. What we can do about it is close the account of somebody using this app for the things listed above. The person who can act fastest is the owner of the space, because removing a member is their power and it takes effect for everybody.

If the person you want to report is the owner, blocking them and leaving the space are yours to do without asking anybody, and writing to us is the other route. Leaving a space does not delete anything: the records stay, and so does the ledger's arithmetic.

10. What blocking does

Blocking is your own decision about what you are willing to look at. It hides that person's profile picture, their notes and their receipt photographs from your screen. Nobody is told, and they stay in the space.

It changes no money. Not an amount, not a transfer, not a balance, yours or anybody else's. That is deliberate: a shared ledger only works if every phone in the space agrees about what there is, and a block that hid a record would make your copy disagree with everyone else's. Blocking is not the same as removing somebody; only the owner of a space can do that.

Your block list is stored on your own account and is readable by nobody but you: not the person blocked, not the other members, not the owner.

11. Removing a member, and closing an account

The owner of a shared space can remove any member. Removing them ends their access at once and keeps every record they wrote, because the balances of everyone else in the space are worked out from those records and would stop adding up without them. A removed member keeps whatever balance they were holding, and the owner can clear it with Clear balance, which records the clearing in the ledger.

We may suspend or close an account that is used for anything under “What is not allowed”, that otherwise materially breaks these terms, or where the law requires it. We may do so without notice where somebody is at risk.
