Features

Every tool for shared money

Pooled is a money tracker for Android. Track what you spend, share the cost of living with the people around you, and keep the whole picture in one place.

Expenses, income, lent & borrowed Spaces for any group Works offline

Expense tracking

Every expense and income entry, kept in one clean history

Pooled keeps a running record of what you spend and what you earn. Each month opens with expenses, income and net side by side, and the analytics tab turns those entries into a picture of where the money actually went.

  • Monthly totals for expenses, income and net
  • Weekly bars that show the spikes and the quiet weeks
  • Category ranking with amounts and each one's share
  • Filter your history down to a single space

Shared spaces

Shared spaces with members, balances and a full ledger

Create a space for flatmates, family or a trip and invite people with a six-character code. Record what each person puts into the space and it becomes spending power they can draw on, and the ledger shows every member with a clear balance as they do.

  • A space for every group, each with its own members and ledger
  • A live balance per member, showing where each one stands
  • Record a payment and both sides of the ledger move together. No money is moved
  • Balances are recalculated from the records, never stored

Budgets, lent & borrowed

Budgets with progress, and every IOU tracked back to zero

Set a limit per category and Pooled fills a progress bar as the month goes on: calm while you are comfortable, red once you pass the line. Money you lent and money you borrowed sits in the same app, so a debt does not quietly become a gift.

  • A progress bar per category, with the amount left spelled out
  • A warning in the app, and on the widget, once a category goes over
  • Log what you lent and what you borrowed, with a reason
  • Record part repayments and watch the balance reach zero

Quick entry

Add an expense in seconds, with the receipt and the note attached

The keypad opens straight on the amount, so a coffee takes three taps. Pick a category, choose the space it belongs to, attach a photo of the receipt and write what it was for. Months later, that note is the answer.

  • A calculator keypad built into the amount field
  • Switch between expense and income with one tap
  • Up to five receipt photos on an entry, stripped of their location data
  • Notes and payment method saved with every entry

And the rest

Smaller things that make Pooled easier to live with

Privacy, portability and a few shortcuts that save you opening the app at all.

App lock with PIN, password and fingerprint unlock Pro

Lock Pooled behind a PIN or a password, and unlock it with your fingerprint. Your money stays yours even when your phone is not in your hand.

CSV and PDF export Pro

Export any date range as a CSV for a spreadsheet, or a PDF with category and payment-method breakdowns. Your records leave with you whenever you want them.

Home-screen widgets

Four Android widgets: balance, budget, quick add and your most-used categories, so the common jobs never need the app open. Balance is free; the other three come with Pro.

Shared grocery lists

A shared space can hold its own lists. Add what you need, tick items off while you are in the shop, and the others see the change.

How billing works

Pro is sold through Google Play and needs a Pooled account. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial; when it ends, Google Play charges the plan you picked automatically unless you cancel before then. It renews each month or year until you cancel in Google Play, and Pro stays until the end of the period you paid for. Prices are in US dollars; Google Play shows the price in your currency before you confirm, and taxes may apply.

The first 100 get 6 months free

The first 100 accounts made since Pro went on sale, with a confirmed email address, get 6 months of Pro free and an OG badge that stays on their profile for good. One place per inbox. Nothing is charged when the 6 months end. Members who joined in early access keep the grant they already have.

For a shared space, the owner's Pro is what counts, so everyone in it gets the bigger limits. Full details are in the Terms of Service, section 6.
