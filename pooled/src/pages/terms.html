We will not pretend otherwise: there is no moderation desk and no queue, and the app does not send reports to us by itself. If the person you want to report is the owner, blocking them and leaving the space are yours to do without asking anybody, and writing to us is the other route. Leaving a space does not delete anything: the records stay, and so does the ledger's arithmetic.

10. What blocking does

Blocking is your own decision about what you are willing to look at. It hides that person's profile picture, their notes and their receipt photographs from your screen. Nobody is told, and they stay in the space.

It changes no money. Not an amount, not a recorded payment, not a balance, yours or anybody else's. That is deliberate: a shared ledger only works if every phone in the space agrees about what there is, and a block that hid a record would make your copy disagree with everyone else's. Blocking is not the same as removing somebody; only the owner of a space can do that.

Your block list is stored on your own account and is readable by nobody but you: not the person blocked, not the other members, not the owner.

11. Removing a member, and closing an account

The owner of a shared space can remove any member. Removing them ends their access at once and keeps every record they wrote, because the balances of everyone else in the space are worked out from those records and would stop adding up without them. A removed member keeps whatever balance they were holding, and the owner can clear it with Clear balance, which records the clearing in the ledger.
