Features

Everything a shared money tracker needs

  • Shared spaces

    A space for the flat, the trip or the family. Everyone adds what they spent, and every member's balance updates for the whole group.

Why we built it

Shared money is where budgeting apps give up

Nearly every money app assumes one person with one wallet. Real life is not like that. Rent is split four ways, somebody else did the shopping, and the ৳500 you lent your brother in March has been quietly forgotten by both of you.

Pooled started as a household ledger that had to reconcile, because the version that did not reconcile caused arguments. Balances are never stored — they are recalculated from the records every time, so the number on your screen can always be traced back to the entries that made it.

It is built by SunnyOrbit, a small independent studio. The same people write the code, answer the email and ship the updates.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Pooled free to use?

Yes. Pooled is in early access and every feature works for everybody. Make an account while early access is open and you also get 6 months of Pro and a permanent OG badge. Nothing is charged, and there is nothing to cancel.

Do I need to connect a bank account?

No. Pooled never asks for bank logins. You add entries by hand in a few taps, so setup stays simple and your accounts stay untouched.

How do shared spaces work?

Create a space for a flat, a trip or a family, invite people with a six-character code, and every entry anyone adds updates the balances the whole group can see.

Can I set budgets and track money I lent?

Yes. Set a monthly limit per category, and log money you lent or borrowed with part repayments until the balance reaches zero.

Is my data private?

Without an account your records never leave your phone, and they are encrypted there. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only company the app talks to is Google: a quick version check when it opens, and sync once you make an account.

Can I export to CSV or PDF?

Yes. Export any date range as a CSV for a spreadsheet, or a PDF with category and payment-method breakdowns.
