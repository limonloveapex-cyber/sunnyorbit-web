Pooled

A shared money tracker for Android

Pooled gives housemates, families and friends one clear view of what has been spent and who paid for it. It works without an account, and without one nothing leaves your phone.

  • Shared spaces for a home, a family or a trip
  • Invite people with a six-character code
  • A live balance for every member of a space
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking

House rules

What every SunnyOrbit app has in common

These are not features we bolt on later. They decide what we are willing to build in the first place.

Privacy first

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your money data is never sold to anyone.

No account needed

Pooled works fully without signing up. With no account, nothing leaves your phone.

No dark patterns

No guilt nudges, no buried settings, no tricks to keep you tapping.

Straight about price

Pooled is free during early access: no price, no subscription, nothing to pay.

One app today, built properly

Pooled is the app we have built so far, and it is the best way to see how we work. Try it with the people you already share money with.
